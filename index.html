🚀 What is PipePipe?

PipePipe is a completely free, open-source app for your Android device. It lets you watch YouTube videos and explore other online services without annoying advertisements, without tracking, and without restrictions. Whether you're tired of ads interrupting your videos, want to play music in the background, or just want a cleaner viewing experience — PipePipe puts you in control.

It’s built by a community of developers who believe that video streaming should be fast, private, and enjoyable. Since it’s open source, anyone can inspect the code, contribute, or modify it. That means no hidden fees, no data harvesting, and no unexpected changes.

🛠️ Key Features

  • 📺 Ad-Free Viewing: Say goodbye to video ads, banner ads, and sponsored interruptions. Watch your content cleanly.
  • 🔇 SponsorBlock Integration: Automatically skips sponsor segments and self-promotional parts in videos. More time watching, less time waiting.
  • 🎵 Background Play: Keep audio playing even when you switch to another app or turn off the screen. Perfect for music, podcasts, or tutorials.
  • 📱 Media Player Built-In: A powerful player that supports different resolutions, play speeds, and subtitle options. Smooth playback anytime.
  • 🌐 Privacy-First: No account needed, no tracking of your viewing habits, and no data leaks. 100% anonymous.
  • 🎨 Simple Interface: Clean design that makes finding videos, playlists, and channels incredibly easy. No clutter.

📋 System Requirements

  • Android Version: 5.0 (Lollipop) or higher
  • Storage: At least 50 MB free space

PipePipe is lightweight and works on most modern Android phones and tablets. No special hardware needed.
